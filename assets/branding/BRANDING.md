# IgboAI brand mark: design rationale

This document records the thinking behind the IgboNLP-Research / IgboAI
visual identity, so the design remains explainable and extensible. Like
everything else in this repository, the branding is reproducible from
source: the images are generated by the scripts in this directory, and
variants should be produced by editing those scripts, not by editing pixels.

## The mark

The centrepiece is a letterform: a lowercase "i" carrying a dot below as
well as the ordinary dot above, with the dot-below rendered in terracotta
as the focal accent.

The dot-below is the most instantly recognisable feature of standard Igbo
orthography. The vowels i̇̀ ọ ụ (dot-below marking the -ATR vowel set) and ṅ
distinguish written Igbo at a glance from other Latin-script text, and
their correct rendering, input, and restoration are themselves recurring
research problems in Igbo NLP. The mark therefore points at the project's
subject down to the level of a single diacritic.

One honesty note, recorded so the symbolism is never over-claimed: the word
"Igbo" itself is spelled with a plain i in standard orthography (tone-marked
Ìgbò, never dotted). The dot-below in the mark and in the wordmark's initial
capital is a deliberate stylistic echo of the orthography's signature
feature, not a spelling of the project's name.

A second reading is intended: the terracotta dot beneath the tall stem is
also the datum under the infrastructure, the small essential thing that the
surrounding machinery exists to serve.

## The surround

The ring of small dots is drawn from the uli tradition: Igbo body and wall
art built from dotted lines, open curves, and asymmetric rhythm. The ring
alternates sizes and colours and is deliberately broken (gaps at the
diagonals) to keep a hand-drawn, non-mechanical feel.

The four node-and-edge motifs on the diagonals carry the AI/NLP half of the
identity: graph edges and network nodes, understated by design so that the
cultural mark remains dominant. The composition states a priority: the
language at the centre, the technology in service around it.

## Palette

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Ground (dark) | deep indigo-black | #1A1826 |
| Line-work | uli cream | #F0E6D2 |
| Accent | terracotta | #C1542C |
| Ground (light variant) | warm off-white | #F7F3EB |

The palette is drawn from uli practice (indigo dye, earth pigments, chalk)
rather than default technology-brand blues. Terracotta is reserved for the
single most important element in any composition (the dot-below); it should
stay scarce to keep its emphasis.

## Files

- `make_avatar.py` -> `avatar_1024.png`, `avatar_512.png`, `avatar_256.png`
  Square mark for the GitHub organisation profile picture and favicons.
- `make_lockup.py` -> `lockup_dark.png`, `lockup_light.png`
  Horizontal wordmark lockups ("IgboAI" with the terracotta dot echo under
  the initial, plus subtitle) for banners, slides, and the README. Use the
  light variant on white/light backgrounds.

## Usage guidance

- Prefer the square mark wherever space is tight; it is designed to survive
  GitHub's circular crop and remain legible at 256 px and below.
- Do not recolour the dot-below; it is the identity's fixed point.
- New variants (favicons, social banners, print) should be added as
  parameters or functions in the generator scripts and committed alongside
  their outputs, keeping the branding reproducible.

## Naming

The wordmark says "IgboAI": the project name, with NLP understood as
subsumed under AI (see ROADMAP.md phases spanning text, speech, and models).
The organisation remains IgboNLP-Research; both names share this identity.

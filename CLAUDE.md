# IgboAI: guidance for Claude

Research focus: AI for the Igbo language (a low-resource, tonal Niger-Congo
language of Nigeria). Core tasks: MT, ASR/TTS, NER, sentiment, LM
pre-training/adaptation, corpus construction, evaluation.

Relevance policy for literature tracking: Igbo-specific work is always in
scope. Other African languages (esp. Nigerian: Yoruba, Hausa, Naija/Pidgin)
are in scope. Non-African work is in scope when the METHOD or FINDING could
transfer to Igbo: tonal modeling, ASR/TTS for low-resource or tonal
languages, data-efficient or cross-lingual training, morphologically rich
language modeling, evaluation methodology, corpus-construction techniques.
A methods paper evaluated only on non-African languages still qualifies if
the technique is language-agnostic. Discard only pure resource releases for
unrelated languages with no methodological contribution. When genuinely
uncertain, KEEP and say so in the Igbo-relevance line — a human reviews
every entry, so false keeps are cheap and false discards are invisible.

Style: summaries are written for NLP researchers; paraphrase, never copy;
be selective rather than exhaustive.

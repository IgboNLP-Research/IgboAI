# IgboAI — guidance for Claude

Research focus: AI for the Igbo language (a low-resource, tonal Niger-Congo
language of Nigeria). Core tasks: MT, ASR/TTS, NER, sentiment, LM
pre-training/adaptation, corpus construction, evaluation.

Relevance policy for literature tracking: Igbo-specific work is always in
scope. Other African languages (esp. Nigerian: Yoruba, Hausa, Naija/Pidgin)
are in scope. Non-African low-resource work is in scope only when the method
or finding transfers to Igbo — tonal modeling, data-efficient training,
cross-lingual transfer, evaluation methodology. Resource-only papers for
unrelated languages are out of scope.

Style: summaries are written for NLP researchers; paraphrase, never copy;
be selective rather than exhaustive.

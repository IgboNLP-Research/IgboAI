- **Generative Fusion Decoding with MambaByte for Low-resource Regional Language Speech Recognition: A Case Study on Javanese and Sundanese** - type: paper · International Journal of Intelligent Engineering and Systems · 2026-08-29 · tags: [asr, method] · languages: [Javanese, Sundanese]
  Fuses a frozen Whisper large-v3 acoustic model with MambaByte, a byte-level state-space language model adapted to each target language purely from text via LoRA continual pretraining, combined at decoding time through generative fusion decoding with a prefix-trie cache that keeps byte-level hypothesis extension tractable on a single GPU. With no labelled speech used for adaptation, this cuts WER by roughly 6-7 percentage points versus zero-shot Whisper on Javanese and Sundanese read speech.
  *Igbo relevance:* offers a concrete recipe for improving Igbo ASR under Whisper's weak zero-shot baseline by adapting a text-only side language model on Igbo text corpora instead of collecting more transcribed speech. <https://doi.org/10.22266/ijies2026.0930.12>

- **Machine Translation for Kusaal: A Parallel Corpus and a First Open-Source System** - type: dataset · Zenodo · 2026-08-28 · tags: [mt, corpus] · languages: [Kusaal, English]
  Assembles a 34,568-pair Kusaal-English parallel corpus from six heterogeneous sources (Bible translation, wordlists, Wikipedia, back-translation) and fine-tunes NLLB-200-distilled-600M for bidirectional translation, initialising the new Kusaal language token from the embedding of its closest NLLB-supported relative (Dagbani) rather than from random noise. The paper also documents two silent data-corruption bugs hit during corpus assembly: an HTML leak in scraped Bible text and a CSV quoting failure that silently dropped rows.
  *Igbo relevance:* the related-language embedding-initialisation trick and the documented silent-corruption pitfalls are directly reusable for Igbo NLLB fine-tuning and for corpus pipelines built from similarly heterogeneous scraped and back-translated sources. <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22144587>

- **Complexity Thresholds and Directional Asymmetry in Low-resource Machine Translation for Banjar-Indonesia Language** - type: paper · International Journal of Intelligent Engineering and Systems · 2026-08-29 · tags: [mt, eval, method] · languages: [Banjar, Indonesian]

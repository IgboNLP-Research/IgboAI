  Runs 80,000 round-trip translation operations across single- and multi-hop pipelines with four systems, from a classical web translator to recent LLMs, scored by BLEU, COMET-Kiwi, xCOMET, and SBERT. Outputs stay fluent while meaning degrades quickly under three distinct decay patterns, and surface overlap metrics mask this loss, a "surface fidelity trap" that also reveals xCOMET over-penalising legitimate paraphrase while under-penalising domain-critical substitutions in legal text.
  *Igbo relevance:* a direct caution against relying on round-trip translation (a common cheap data-augmentation trick) for Igbo MT or paraphrase data without an independent, paraphrase-aware quality check, since fluent-but-wrong output would otherwise pass unnoticed. <https://doi.org/10.3846/ntcs.2026.26227>

## 2026-09-07

- **voicedata/final_igbo** - type: dataset · Hugging Face · 2026-09-07 · tags: [asr, tts, corpus] · languages: [Igbo]
  A Hugging Face dataset release pairing Igbo audio and text at a scale in the 100K-1M row band, distributed as Parquet with dask/polars/mlcroissant loader support. No dataset card or methodology description is published yet beyond the catalogue tags, so provenance, recording conditions, and transcription quality are unverified.
  *Igbo relevance:* a sizeable Igbo speech-text pairing is directly usable as ASR/TTS training or fine-tuning data if quality checks (speaker diversity, transcription accuracy, tonal marking) confirm the catalogue's scale claim. <https://huggingface.co/datasets/voicedata/final_igbo>

- **RAÍZ: A Hybrid Architecture Based on Linguistic Knowledge and Deep Learning for the Machine Translation of Low-Resource Indigenous Languages** - type: paper · International Journal of Combinatorial Optimization Problems and Informatics · 2026-09-06 · tags: [mt, method] · languages: [Spanish, Nahuatl, Mazahua]
  Combines a Transformer backbone with linguistically informed components (phonetic normalisation, morphological segmentation, lexical retrieval, community-centred validation with native speakers) for Spanish-Nahuatl and Spanish-Mazahua translation, reporting 27-29 BLEU and 55-57 chrF, evaluated with a mix of automatic metrics and qualitative naturalness/cultural-fidelity judging.
  *Igbo relevance:* the hybrid strategy of pairing morphological segmentation and phonetic normalisation with a standard Transformer, plus native-speaker-in-the-loop validation, is a transferable template for Igbo MT systems that need to handle tone and agglutination without large parallel corpora. <https://doi.org/10.61467/2007.1558.2026.v17i5.1451>

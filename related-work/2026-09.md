- **Uncertainty-Aware Bayesian Federated Learning for Low-Resource Neural Machine Translation at the Edge** - type: paper · Figshare · 2026-09-04 · tags: [mt, method] · languages: [unspecified low-resource pairs]
  Proposes MT-BayesFL, a multitask federated-learning framework for on-device MT that pairs a shared lightweight encoder with per-task decoders, places matrix-normal posteriors over the shared attention projections for calibrated uncertainty, and aggregates client updates on the server with covariance-aware weighting rather than plain averaging. The framing targets edge deployments where compute, data locality, and weak low-resource-pair performance all bind at once.
  *Igbo relevance:* federated aggregation with uncertainty-aware weighting is a plausible way to pool Igbo parallel data scattered across institutions or devices without centralising it, and the calibrated-uncertainty angle could help flag low-confidence Igbo translations for human review. <https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.33433066.v1>

- **Foundations and Frontiers of Swahili Natural Language Processing** - type: paper · African Journal of Mathematics and Statistics Studies · 2026-09-04 · tags: [asr, sentiment, llm, corpus, eval] · languages: [Swahili]
  A systematic review of the Swahili NLP landscape covering labelled datasets for sentiment analysis, ASR, and summarisation, and the tokenisation and fine-tuning behaviour of dedicated models (SwahBERT, AfriBERTa) and general LLMs against Swahili's agglutinative morphology. It highlights a "resource quality gap": thin domain-specific corpora in healthcare and law and little dialectal or fine-grained annotation coverage, and calls for unified multi-task benchmarks.
  *Igbo relevance:* Swahili's trajectory from data scarcity to a partial tooling ecosystem, and its diagnosed gaps (domain corpora, dialect coverage, missing shared benchmarks), is a close analogue for auditing where Igbo NLP resourcing currently stands and what a comparable benchmark suite should prioritise. <https://doi.org/10.52589/ajmss-qdf2cvcu>

- **KOBONGOLA-Lite: Design and Prospective Evaluation Protocol for a Hybrid Offline French-Lingala Neural Translator on Resource-Constrained Smartphones** - type: paper · International Journal of Innovative Science and Research Technology (IJISRT) · 2026-08-31 · tags: [mt, method, eval] · languages: [French, Lingala]
  Specifies, but does not yet implement, a bidirectional offline French-Lingala NMT system for entry-level Android phones, combining a stratified Congolese corpus, explicit handling of French-Lingala code-switching, a context-gated lexical fallback mechanism, and on-device validation across latency, memory, and thermal behaviour. The proposed evaluation protocol pairs chrF++/SacreBLEU/AfriCOMET with blinded human assessment and a pre-registered, multi-test statistical correction, while explicitly disclaiming any implementation results at this stage.
  *Igbo relevance:* both the offline/on-device deployment constraints and the pre-registered, multi-metric evaluation protocol (automatic metrics plus corrected human assessment) are directly reusable templates for building and rigorously evaluating an offline English-Igbo or Igbo-Pidgin mobile translator. <https://doi.org/10.38124/ijisrt/26aug847>

- **Artificial Intelligence and Indigenous Language Revitalization: A Gadé Language Digitization Model** - type: paper · Journal of Artificial Intelligence & Cloud Computing · 2026-08-30 · tags: [asr, mt, corpus, method] · languages: [Gadé]
  Proposes an AI framework for digitizing and revitalizing Gadé, a tone-rich Niger-Congo language of North-Central Nigeria, combining a tone-aware ASR component that maps pitch contours onto native orthographic tone markers with an NMT system using subword tokenization suited to Niger-Congo morphology, built on a corpus annotated with native speakers and local language institutes. Reports word-error-rate and translation-quality gains over baseline multilingual models, though given the venue these numbers warrant independent verification.
  *Igbo relevance:* Gadé's profile (tonal, Niger-Congo, under-resourced, Nigerian) closely mirrors Igbo's, so the pitch-contour-to-tone-marker ASR mapping and the Niger-Congo-tailored subword tokenization are concrete design choices worth prototyping for Igbo ASR and MT. <https://doi.org/10.47363/jaicc/2026(5)535>

- **Iconic-systematicity in Guangzhou Cantonese Reduplicative Ideophones** - type: paper · Journal of Artificial Intelligence and Information · 2026-08-30 · tags: [method] · languages: [Cantonese]
  Tests whether Guangzhou Cantonese ABB-pattern ideophones show iconic sound-meaning correspondences, coding 181 ideophones on 82 phonological features and modelling their relation to dictionary-derived semantic embeddings with PLS regression and Random Forest. Global models fail to predict the overall semantic space, but targeted tests find the vowel /i/ reliably marks smallness and that /t/ marks smallness only in coda position, not onset.
  *Igbo relevance:* this is linguistics rather than a system paper, but its statistical framework for testing sound-symbolism in a tonal language is a transferable method for probing whether Igbo's own extensive ideophone inventory carries similar phoneme-position-sensitive iconicity, which could inform tone-aware lexicon or TTS prosody work. <https://doi.org/10.66069/ojspub.1811260811>
